How the max offer is worked out

Start with what the house should sell for once fixed. Take off selling costs, repairs, holding costs and your target profit. What remains has to cover the purchase price plus the costs that grow with the price: closing costs, loan interest and points. Dividing by those gives the highest price that still works.

Why not just use the 70% rule?

The 70% rule says to pay no more than 70% of the after-repair value, minus repairs. It's a fast screen, but it ignores your real loan, how long you hold the house and your selling costs. This calculator shows both, so you can see how far apart they are.

What people get wrong

The after-repair value and the repair budget drive almost everything here. Base the value on recent nearby sales, and get real repair prices when you can. A small miss on either one can wipe out the profit.
