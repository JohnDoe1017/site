How it works

You add up everything you spent to buy and fix the house. The new loan is the appraised value times the lender's limit. After refinance costs, that loan pays you back. Whatever it doesn't cover is the cash left in the deal.

Watch the cash flow

Pulling all your money out can leave a bigger loan payment than the rent can carry. Check the monthly cash flow here as closely as the cash you get back. A negative number means the house costs you money every month.
