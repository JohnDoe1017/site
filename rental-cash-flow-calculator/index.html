What the results mean

Cash flow is what's left each month after expenses and the loan payment. Cash-on-cash return is a year of cash flow divided by the cash you put in. Cap rate is a year of income before the loan, divided by the price, so it compares properties regardless of financing. Debt coverage is income before the loan divided by the loan payment. Lenders often want 1.20 or higher.

The costs people leave out

Rent minus the mortgage isn't cash flow. Empty months, repairs and big replacements like a roof or water heater all come out of the rent, even in months when nothing breaks. Setting aside a share of rent for them gives a truer picture.
